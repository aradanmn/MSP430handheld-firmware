The Register Office

Sixteen registers, one building. Picture R0–R3 as the building's specialists doing one job each — that picture is the whole point of this page, not decoration on top of a fact you already had.

The rest of the building, briefly: R3 is the vending machine that hands out 0, 1, 2 or −1 without anyone fetching them (the constant generator). R4–R11 are permanent desks — whoever sits there stays put across a call. R12–R15 are one shared scratch table: assume it's wiped clean every time you walk back into a room.
